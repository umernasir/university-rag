UNIVERSITY ADMISSIONS

Undergraduate Admission

The university offers undergraduate programs in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Information Technology.

Eligibility

Applicants for undergraduate programs must have completed Intermediate, A-Levels, or an equivalent qualification recognized by the relevant education authority.

For BS Computer Science, BS Software Engineering, BS Data Science, BS Artificial Intelligence, and BS Information Technology, applicants must have at least 60% marks in Intermediate or an equivalent qualification.

Applicants must meet the specific subject requirements of their selected program.

Admission Process

The undergraduate admission process consists of the following steps:

1. Complete the online admission application.
2. Upload the required academic and identification documents.
3. Pay the application processing fee.
4. Appear in the admission test if required by the selected program.
5. Check the merit list after the admission deadline.
6. Complete fee payment and enrollment if selected.

Required Documents

Applicants may be required to provide:

- Intermediate or equivalent certificate/result card
- Matriculation or equivalent certificate
- CNIC or B-Form
- Passport-size photographs
- Domicile, where applicable
- Admission application form
- Admission test result, where applicable

Admission Deadline

Applicants must submit their application before the admission deadline announced by the university for the relevant intake. Applications submitted after the deadline may not be considered.

Admission Test

Some undergraduate programs may require an admission test. Test requirements depend on the selected program and admission intake.

Merit and Selection

Admission is subject to eligibility requirements, available seats, merit, and any applicable admission test requirements.

International Applicants

International applicants may need to provide equivalent educational qualifications and additional documents required by the university.

Admissions Office

Applicants should contact the university admissions office for the latest information about admission deadlines, eligibility requirements, application status, and required documents.
