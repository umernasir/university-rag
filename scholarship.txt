UNIVERSITY SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

The university provides different scholarship and financial assistance opportunities for eligible students. Scholarship availability, eligibility criteria, and award amounts may vary by academic year.

Merit-Based Scholarship

Merit-based scholarships may be awarded to students who demonstrate strong academic performance.

Eligibility may depend on:

- Academic marks
- Admission merit
- Semester GPA
- Program requirements

Need-Based Financial Assistance

Students facing financial difficulties may apply for need-based financial assistance.

Applicants may be required to provide financial information and supporting documents to demonstrate their financial need.

Academic Performance Requirement

Students receiving academic scholarships may be required to maintain a specified academic performance level.

Failure to meet the required academic performance may result in the scholarship being reduced, suspended, or discontinued according to university policy.

Application Process

Students seeking scholarships should:

1. Review the eligibility requirements.
2. Complete the scholarship application form.
3. Submit the required supporting documents.
4. Submit the application before the announced deadline.
5. Wait for the scholarship committee's decision.

Required Documents

Depending on the scholarship type, applicants may be asked to provide:

- Academic transcripts
- Admission or enrollment documents
- CNIC or B-Form
- Proof of income
- Financial documents
- Scholarship application form

Scholarship Deadline

Scholarship applications must be submitted before the deadline announced by the university.

Late applications may not be considered.

Scholarship Amount

The scholarship amount depends on the scholarship category and the student's eligibility. A scholarship may cover part or all of the applicable tuition fee depending on the approved award.

Important Notice

Scholarship policies, eligibility requirements, deadlines, and award amounts may change. Students should verify the latest scholarship information with the university's official administration or financial aid office.
